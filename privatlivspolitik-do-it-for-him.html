Privacy Policy

for the app Do It For Him · last updated 18 August 2026
Do It For Him is a habit tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is kept, and who can see it. It is short, because there is very little to explain.
In short: the app has no network connection at all. It does not collect, transmit or receive anything. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. Everything you enter stays on your own iPhone.

1. Who is responsible?

Kristine Sune Thestrup Ehlers
Contact: kristine+app@ehlers.me

Do It For Him is made by one person. If you have a question about this policy, write to the address above.

2. What the app stores on your device

Everything below is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent anywhere.

  • The name you enter during setup, so the character can use it. You can leave it blank, and the app will use a pet name instead.
  • How you would like to be addressed, which decides one word in some of the lines.
  • The mood you have chosen, for example Commanding or Devoted.
  • Which habits you have turned on, any habits you have added yourself, and any daily goals you have changed.
  • Today's tally for each habit, which resets when the date changes.
  • Your streak, your best streak, and the dates on which you completed every habit you had chosen.
  • Your reminder setting, meaning whether the daily reminder is on and what time you picked.

3. What the app does not do

  • It has no network code. There is no server to talk to, and the app works exactly the same in airplane mode.
  • There is no account and no sign-in, so there is nothing to tie your habits to a person.
  • There are no advertisements and no advertising identifiers.
  • There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone, because nothing leaves your device in the first place.

This is why the app's App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

4. Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and off until you turn it on. When it is on, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and then schedules them on your own device, up to two weeks ahead. The text is one of the character's lines and may include the name you entered, and it is put together on your iPhone. No push server is involved, and nothing about the reminder is sent to me or to anyone else.

You can turn the reminder off in the app, or withdraw the permission entirely under Settings → Notifications → Do It For Him on your iPhone.

5. How long is it kept?

Your data stays on your iPhone until you change it or delete it. Deleting the app removes all of it, and there is no copy anywhere else, because no copy was ever made.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup may include this app's data, in the same way it includes data from your other apps. That backup is between you and Apple, and I have no access to it.

6. Children

Do It For Him is rated 17+ and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly store information about children.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access to your personal data, and the right to have it corrected or erased. Because the app has no network connection and no account, I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to hand over or delete. You have all of it on your own device, and you can view, change or erase it in the app or by deleting the app.

If you believe your data is being handled incorrectly, you have the right to complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy is updated here and the date at the top changes with it. This page is the current version.
