Do It For Him

Book-boyfriend habit tracker
Submitted to the App Store and under review
Do It For Him is a habit tracker with a character in it. He notices every time you drink your water, move your body, tidy up or get to bed on time, and he has something to say about it. The tone is enemies-to-lovers and he is morally grey. The rest is ordinary self-care: water, sleep and the washing up.

For fans of enemies-to-lovers and morally-grey romance. 16+.

What the app does

Four moods

You choose the tone he speaks in, and you can switch whenever you like.

CommandingIn control, possessive, expects the best of you.
FlirtyTeasing, witty, impossible to ignore.
DevotedTender, adoring, endlessly proud of you.
ProudWarm, encouraging, your biggest supporter.

There are around 120 lines for each mood, roughly 480 in all, and the app works through the whole pool before anything repeats. There are separate lines for morning, evening and night, for each individual habit, and for the days you hit a milestone in your streak.

Only the habits you want

You turn habits on and off as you like, and you can add your own. If you do not want a habit, you remove it completely. There is no "skipped" and no red marks for something you never asked for.

Some habits are counted several times a day, for example eight glasses of water or three meals, and you can set the number up or down yourself.

Streak

A day is full when everything you have chosen is done. The app keeps track of your current streak, your best one, and which days you closed.

He uses your name

You enter your first name during setup and choose how you would like to be addressed. Your name then turns up in his lines now and again. If you would rather not enter one, he calls you something else and everything works as before.

A reminder, if you want one

You can turn on a daily reminder and pick the time yourself. It is scheduled on your own phone, and the text is one of his lines. You can turn it off again in the app or in your iPhone settings.

No account, and nothing leaves your phone

There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party services. The app has no network connection at all. Everything you enter and choose stays on your own phone. The details are in the privacy policy.

The character is original and deliberately has no name. He is only ever addressed by pet names. The app uses no names, places or designs from anyone else's work.
